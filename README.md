# 📦 Inventory & Supply Chain Management Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes inventory and supply chain operations using **Microsoft Power BI**. The goal is to transform supply chain data into meaningful business insights related to sales, inventory, warehouses, transportation, lead times, and backorders.

The interactive Power BI dashboard allows users to analyze supply chain performance by **Region** and **Category** and provides a comprehensive view of inventory and operational performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Effective inventory and supply chain management is essential for reducing operational costs, avoiding stockouts, improving warehouse utilization, and ensuring timely order fulfillment.

This project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* How are daily sales and inventory levels performing?
* How efficiently are warehouses being utilized?
* What is the inventory turnover?
* Which regions and categories have higher transportation costs?
* How do annual unit sales change over time?
* Which product categories have longer lead times?
* How many backorders exist by order status?
* How is inventory distributed across regions and categories?

---

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard contains interactive KPIs, charts, and filters for analyzing inventory and supply chain performance.

### Filters

* **Region**
* **Category**

### Key KPIs

* Daily Sales / Units Sold
* Inventory Level
* Warehouse Utilization
* Inventory Turnover
* Transportation Cost
* Lead Time
* Backorders

### Visualizations

#### 1. Warehouse Utilization

Analyzes warehouse capacity utilization and helps identify warehouses with high or low utilization.

#### 2. Transportation Cost by Region and Category

Compares transportation expenses across different regions and product categories.

#### 3. Sum of Units Sold by Year

Shows yearly sales volume and helps identify changes in demand over time.

#### 4. Average Lead Time by Category

Compares average order lead time across product categories.

#### 5. Count of Backorders by Order Status

Analyzes backorders according to order status to identify potential supply chain issues.

#### 6. Sum of Inventory Level by Category and Region

Shows how inventory is distributed across different categories and regions.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. Monitor warehouses with consistently high utilization to prevent capacity constraints.
2. Investigate regions and categories with high transportation costs.
3. Align inventory levels with demand to reduce excess inventory and stockout risk.
4. Investigate categories with longer lead times to identify supply chain bottlenecks.
5. Monitor backorders and identify their underlying causes.
6. Use regional and category-level analysis to improve inventory allocation.
